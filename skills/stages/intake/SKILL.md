# Intake Notes

Use only when the human asks to organize exploratory discussion without entering the governed workflow. Capture intent, motivation, alternatives, apparent decisions, assumptions, and questions, clearly separating what was decided from what was merely discussed. Keep the notes in the conversation unless the human requests a separate document.

Do not create workflow artifacts, assign a workflow ID, invoke stage agents, or treat discussion as approval. When the human explicitly initiates a Handoff, transfer to the Handoff stage instructions.
